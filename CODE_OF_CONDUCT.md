# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where anyone can report a problem or propose a change without
being treated badly. We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of
experience, identity, background or language.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about the code and kind about the person.
- Assume the other side has context you lack.
- Accept a clear "no" on scope: the pill stays compact and the plugin keeps one data source.
- Give and take technical criticism without turning it into a judgement of someone's worth.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, sexualised attention, or comments about a person's identity.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or dismissing a report without reading it.
- Any conduct that would be obviously inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space the project uses, and to
behaviour outside those spaces when it directly affects them.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily block
or permanently block a participant. Decisions are made in private and explained to the person
concerned.

Report a problem to the maintainers through a private channel listed on the repository, such as a
security advisory addressed to the maintainers. Reports are handled confidentially.
